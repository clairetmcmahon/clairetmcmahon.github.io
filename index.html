Claire McMahon

I am a PhD student in economics at Harvard University. My research explores how market shaping can encourage innovation for global health and pandemic preparedness.

Previously, I was a research professional at the Market Shaping Accelerator at the University of Chicago. I graduated from Dartmouth College with a BA in Mathematics and Economics.
